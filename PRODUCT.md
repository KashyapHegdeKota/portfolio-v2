# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary audience is both:

- Recruiters and hiring managers evaluating Kashyap Hegde Kota for internship and full-time opportunities.
- Potential collaborators and clients assessing technical depth, product judgment, and fit for future work.

## Product Purpose

This portfolio presents Kashyap's engineering work, experience, and technical writing. Its primary outcome is to secure an internship or full-time role by giving visitors credible evidence of AI product engineering ability and a direct path to review the resume, explore projects, and make contact.

## Positioning

Kashyap is positioned as an AI product engineer who combines applied AI, full-stack systems, cloud infrastructure, and thoughtful product execution. The portfolio should demonstrate both engineering substance and the ability to turn technical systems into usable products.

## Operating Context

Visitors evaluate the portfolio through the homepage, project links, engineering journal, downloadable resume, GitHub profile, LinkedIn profile, and direct email contact. Recruiters and hiring managers may scan quickly before choosing whether to inspect a project or resume in depth.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The site is a web portfolio implemented with Next.js App Router, React, Tailwind CSS, and Framer Motion.
- The homepage covers introduction, technical capabilities, projects, experience, and contact information.
- The local MDX blog provides engineering notes and project build logs.
- The portfolio must preserve AI product engineering as the central professional position.
- Availability for internship and full-time roles must remain clear.
- Resume claims and project metrics must remain factual and consistent with their source material; future work must not embellish or fabricate them.

## Brand Commitments

- Use the name Kashyap Hegde Kota.
- Preserve the professional identity of AI product engineer.
- Keep availability visible and credible.
- Maintain a technically fluent, direct voice that demonstrates product judgment without overstating evidence.

## Evidence on Hand

- Resume: `website/public/resume.pdf`
- Portrait: `website/public/Kashyap picture.jpg`
- Project facts, metrics, links, and supporting images: `website/src/data/projects.js` and `website/public/`
- Experience and education claims: `website/src/components/ExperienceTimeline.js`
- Engineering writing: `website/src/content/blog/`
- GitHub and LinkedIn links are present in the portfolio source.
- No testimonials, client endorsements, hiring outcomes, or third-party awards were confirmed; future work must not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

1. Lead with hiring relevance: make role fit, capabilities, proof, and availability quick to understand.
2. Demonstrate, do not merely claim: connect professional positioning to real projects, metrics, writing, and source links.
3. Preserve factual integrity: treat the resume and project records as authoritative evidence.
4. Serve different evaluation depths: support fast recruiter scanning and deeper technical review.
5. Make contact frictionless once the work has established credibility.
